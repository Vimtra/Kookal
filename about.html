About The Windgrove

The Windgrove, Kotagiri

The Windgrove is more than just a real estate project, it's a thoughtfully planned opportunity to own land in Kotagiri, one of the most serene and scenic hill stations in Tamil Nadu. Located 6,400 feet above sea level in the heart of the Nilgiri Hills, The Windgrove offers premium plots for sale in Kotagiri, just 30 km from Ooty and only 2.7 km from the picturesque Uyilatti Falls.

Spread across 14 acres of pristine landscape, The Windgrove is surrounded by high-yielding farmlands and lush Western Ghats greenery, making it the perfect location for those looking to build a holiday home, organic farm, or eco-resort in Kotagiri. Each of the 52 well-defined plots offers privacy, panoramic views, and access to fresh mountain air and clean spring water — a rare combination of natural beauty and investment potential.

At The Windgrove, we believe in sustainable living, clean energy, and creating legacies. With increasing demand for hill station plots in Tamil Nadu, The Windgrove gives you the rare opportunity to own land in the Nilgiris that holds long-term value — both emotionally and financially. The Windgrove invites you to experience a better way of life: connected, and future-forward.
