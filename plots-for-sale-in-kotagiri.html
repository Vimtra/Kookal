About The Windgrove

The Windgrove is more than just a real estate project, it's a thoughtfully planned opportunity to own land in Kotagiri, one of the most serene and scenic hill stations in Tamil Nadu. Located 6,400 feet above sea level in the heart of the Nilgiri Hills, The Windgrove offers premium plots for sale in Kotagiri, just 30 km from Ooty and only 2.7 km from the picturesque Uyilatti Falls.

  • Prime Location – A scenic, high-potential investment destination.
  • Future-Ready Planning – Thoughtfully planned layouts for lifestyle and value.
  • High ROI Potential – Hill station plots with high growth potential.
  • Luxury in Nature – Premium community surrounded by nature.

Scenic view hill top residential plots for sale in Kotagiri near the famous Uyillati Water Falls. These plots are located inside 'The Windgrove' gated community with world class amenities including concrete internal roads, street lights, 24 x 7 security guards, CCTV network, lawn, garden, etc. This gated community project in Kotagiri is nestled high in the lush Western Ghats, just 20 minutes drive from Ooty and Coonoor. Positioned at an altitude of 6,400 feet above sea level, The Windgrove offers 52 premium plots spread across 15 acres of unspoiled beauty. Whether you're dreaming of a holiday home, a luxury villa, an eco-resort, or an organic farm, this is where your vision takes root.
